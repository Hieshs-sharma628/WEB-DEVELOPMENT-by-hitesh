Google LLC is an American multinational technology company and one of the world's most valuable brands.

Founded in 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin at Stanford University. Sundar Pichai is the current CEO.

Key products include Google Search, Gmail, YouTube, Chrome, Android, Maps, and AI tools like Gemini.

History

Google started as a research project in 1996. The name came from a misspelling of "googol" (1 followed by 100 zeros).

Early years

Initially funded by a $100,000 investment from Andy Bechtolsheim in 1998. By 1999, it raised $25 million from Kleiner Perkins and Sequoia Capital.
